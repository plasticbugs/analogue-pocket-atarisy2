Super Sprint (Atari Games, 1986)

Atari's three-player overhead racer
on the System 2 board, all of it in
gateware:

* DEC T-11 at 10 MHz, game logic,
  with the 137412-108 slapstic
* playfield, alphanumerics and
  motion objects at 512x384
* sound board: 6502, two POKEYs,
  YM2151 stereo

Controls
  D-pad / stick   steer
  A, B, X, Y      accelerator
  Select          coin
  Start           start
  Dock pads 2, 3  players 2, 3

ROMs are not included. Build the
image from your own MAME ssprint
romset:

  python3 mra_build.py ssprint.mra
      ssprint.zip

and copy ssprint.rom into
Assets/ssprint/common/.
